\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper draft introduces Agentic MetaSpiderMark, an agentic task scheduler for meta-learning-based diffusion watermark verification.
The approach keeps the original SpiderMark watermark injection mechanism unchanged while using online verifier feedback to adaptively schedule downstream attack episodes.
Preliminary results show consistent gains over a non-meta verifier across nine transformations, suggesting that verifier robustness depends not only on architecture and watermark geometry, but also on closed-loop task exposure during training.
The next step is a controlled scheduler benchmark that evaluates the agentic AI scheduler against uniform sampling, UCB bandits, ATS-style adaptive scheduling, BASS-style contextual bandits, and Adaptive Sampler-style task sampling under matched attacks, checkpoint rules, and compute budgets.
After the scheduler comparison is stable, a fixed-scheduler SOTA/canonical meta-learning comparison should evaluate FOMAML, MAML, ANIL, Reptile, Matching Networks, Prototypical Networks, and an R2D2-style ridge solver.
