\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper introduces Agentic MetaSpiderMark, an agentic task scheduler for meta-learning-based diffusion watermark verification.
The approach keeps the SpiderMark watermark injection mechanism unchanged while using online verifier feedback to adaptively schedule downstream attack episodes.
Under matched 2,000-step meta-training, shared downstream batches, and accuracy-selected checkpoints, Agentic MetaSpiderMark achieves the highest mean fixed-threshold accuracy on the unseen 1,000-image COCO test set: 89.15\% across eight attacks.
No competing scheduler achieves a higher mean accuracy, and paired analysis confirms positive gains over uniform sampling, Adaptive Sampler, and Bandit-UCB.
The smaller differences from BASS and ATS remain unresolved, while Uniform retains a slight AUROC advantage.
For the operational objective of binary watermark verification, these results establish Agentic MetaSpiderMark as the best accuracy-first scheduler in the controlled benchmark, while deliberately avoiding a claim of universal dominance across attacks or metrics.
Future work should replicate the controlled comparison across training seeds and evaluate canonical meta-learning updates, scheduler overhead, and additional held-out distributions.
